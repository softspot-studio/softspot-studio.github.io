Bring a little of their fur

Fur is what makes a piece theirs. We felt it into the top coat of a mini, or seal it inside a charm.

How to collect fur

Brush your pet and keep the loose fur. A small handful is enough for a charm; a mini needs a bit more. Make sure it's clean and fully dry, then put it in a zip bag or envelope with your pet's name.

No fur?

That's fine. We match their colours with wool instead.
